The idea

Somewhere, another you made the other choice.

One text you almost didn't send. One door you almost didn't walk through. One morning you almost stayed in bed. Every life is a quiet multiverse of the choices you nearly made — and PARALLELS lets you step inside them. Not to regret the past, but to feel, in your gut, that the road you're on is yours, and it isn't finished yet.
